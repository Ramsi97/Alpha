The word list in words.js (both WORDLE_GUESSES and the words WORDLE_ANSWERS
was curated from) is derived from SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists),
via its "american-english" package (Debian's wamerican, itself built from
SCOWL). Reproduced below is the relevant permission notice from that
package's copyright file, as required by its terms.

WORDLE_GUESSES is the unmodified set of 5-letter entries extracted from
that word list. WORDLE_ANSWERS is a hand-curated subset of
WORDLE_GUESSES, selected by the AlphaGo project for everyday
recognizability as a Wordle-style daily answer (see CREDITS.md) — this
selection work is original to this project, layered on top of the SCOWL
list under the same permissive terms above.
