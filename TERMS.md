## What this is

AlphaGo is a casual-games hub inside a Telegram Mini App. You earn XP, levels, achievements, and leaderboard rank by playing games, checking in daily, completing tasks, and inviting friends. There is no real-money economy: XP, levels, and badges have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, or withdrawn.

## Fair play

- Scores are validated server-side (minimum play time, maximum plausible score, and a statistical check against other players' scores for the same game). Implausible scores are held and reviewed rather than immediately rejected, in case of a false positive.
- We may ban an account or reverse XP (via a ledger entry, never silently) for cheating, abuse of referrals/challenges/tasks, or abusive behavior toward other players.
- Don't try to manipulate rate limits, create multiple accounts to abuse referral/task rewards, or interfere with other players' sessions.

## Sponsored content

Some tasks may be sponsored. Completing one is optional and clearly marked as sponsored where the app shows it.

## Your account
